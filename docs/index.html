Schoology Grade Checker

Three steps, and the last one takes seconds.

You set it up once. After that, checking your grades is a single click.

Install it

Download the installer for your computer and run it. The installer opens Setup for you when it's done.

Choose your classes

Sign in once in Setup. It finds your classes on Schoology and you tick the ones you want. That's it.

Check your grades

Open Schoology Grades any time. A small report window shows every class, and it's usually ready in under ten seconds.

Small, quick, and easy on the eyes.

Everything it does is in service of getting you the number you came for.

Every class at once

All your classes load side by side instead of one after another, so checking six classes takes only a little longer than checking one.

Light, dark, or automatic

The report follows your computer's theme, and a little sun/moon button in its corner lets you pick. It remembers your choice.

It finds your classes

No copying links. Setup signs in, lists the classes on your Schoology dashboard, and you just click the ones you want.

Problems in plain words

If something goes wrong, it tells you what happened and what to click: "Can't reach the internet", "The sign-in didn't work", and so on.

Stays on your computer

There's no account and no server of ours. It talks only to Schoology and your school's sign-in, to GitHub (to check for updates), and to Google (for a small Chrome helper).

Exact percent, familiar letters

You see the percent to the hundredth, just as Schoology shows it. The letter grade is worked out from the rounded percent.

Install it.

Python isn't needed. You do need Google Chrome, because that's how the program signs in for you.

Windows 10 or 11

Download the installer
  1. Open the downloaded file. If Windows says it "protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. (The program isn't signed with a paid certificate, so Windows is cautious.)
  2. Follow the steps. You don't need administrator rights.
  3. Setup opens at the end. Sign in and pick your classes.

Prefer not to install? Download the portable version: two programs you can run from any folder.

Mac (Apple Silicon)

Download the disk image
  1. Open the .dmg and drag both apps onto the Applications folder.
  2. Open Schoology Setup. macOS blocks it the first time, because the apps aren't signed with a paid Apple certificate. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  3. Do the same for Schoology Grades, then pick your classes in Setup.

For M1 chips and newer. Not sure? Apple menu → About This Mac.

What happens to my password?

Straight answers, because it's a fair question.

How it works

  • The program signs in to Schoology the way you would: in a hidden Chrome window, on your own computer.
  • It connects to your school's Schoology site and the sign-in page your school uses, to github.com to see whether a newer version exists, and (the first time, and after Chrome updates) to Google to download the small helper that lets the program control Chrome. There's nothing else, and no analytics.
  • Your email and password are saved in a file called config.json in the program's data folder, as plain text. So use it only on a computer you trust, and don't share that folder.

Good to know

  • It's unofficial. It isn't made by, or connected to, Schoology, PowerSchool or any school. Your school's own gradebook is the official record.
  • Check your school's rules on automated tools before you use it.
  • To remove everything, uninstall the program and delete its data folder. That erases the saved sign-in.

Questions

My school wants me to approve the sign-in on my phone.

In Setup, tick "Show the browser while signing in". A Chrome window opens so you can approve the sign-in once, and after that it usually remembers you.

The grades look different from my school's gradebook.

The program shows what Schoology shows right now. The letter grade is worked out from the rounded percent using a standard scale, so it's an estimate. Your school's gradebook is the official record.

Does it work on an Intel Mac, Linux, or a Chromebook?

Not right now. The Mac download is for Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and newer), and the other download is for Windows 10 and 11.

I changed classes. How do I update the list?

Open Schoology Setup again, sign in, and tick your new classes. Your old choices stay ticked unless you change them.

How do I know when there's a new version?

A banner appears at the top of the report. Download the new installer and run it over the old one. Your saved sign-in and classes are kept.

It says Chrome couldn't start.

Click Try again. If it keeps happening, restart your computer. If the program is in a folder that OneDrive syncs, pausing OneDrive or moving the folder usually fixes it.

On a Mac it says it can't find my settings.

That happens when the apps are opened straight from the Downloads folder or the disk image. Drag both apps into Applications first, then open them from there.

How do I uninstall it?
  • Windows: Settings → Apps, find Schoology Grade Checker, and click Uninstall. It asks whether to keep your saved settings.
  • Mac: drag both apps to the Trash, and delete the SchoologyGrades folder in ~/Library/Application Support if you want the saved settings gone too.
